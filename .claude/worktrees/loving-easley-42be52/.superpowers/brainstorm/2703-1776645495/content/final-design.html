Day of Wire Workflow
Day Before
Email Cashiering with the expected wire amount and plan name. Email the prior record keeper to confirm. Both parties should be on notice going into liquidation day.
Wire Confirmation
Cashiering pulls wires at 7 AM, 9 AM, 1 PM, and 3 PM CST. They notify the DC by 3:45 PM if the wire was received. Wires after 3 PM CST deposit the next business day.
The SSBT cut-off is 4:00 PM EST — the Fund Management Calendar must be updated and the notification email sent before this time on any day trades go out.
Pre-Checks Before Running Day of Wire
  • Plan fully set up in P3
  • Trans ref numbers created (one per fund)
  • CONV file totals match vendor breakdown
  • Dummy participant exists in census (mapping only)
  • Fund mapping approved and loaded
