Liquidation Day
liquidation day-of-wire wire cashiering critical
The day the prior record keeper liquidates plan assets and sends them to Transamerica. The highest-stakes day in the conversion — every action is time-sensitive and sequential.
Day Before
Email Cashiering with the expected wire amount and plan name. Email the prior record keeper to confirm everything is on track. Both parties should be on notice.
Morning of Liquidation Day
Send a go-day email to the prior RK. Once the vendor confirms the wire is sent, notify Cashiering immediately.
Wires received after 3:00 PM CST are deposited the next business day. The SSBT cut-off is 4:00 PM EST — the Fund Management Calendar must be updated before this time.
Pre-Checks Before Running Day of Wire
  • Plan is fully set up in P3
  • Trans ref numbers created (one per fund)
  • CONV file totals match vendor breakdown
  • Dummy participant exists in census (mapping only)
  • Fund mapping is approved and loaded
