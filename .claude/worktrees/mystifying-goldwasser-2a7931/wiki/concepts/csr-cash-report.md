# CSR Cash Report (2025)

The 2025 sponsor-facing Conversion Summary Report variant used for **cash conversions** — conversions where prior-vendor assets are liquidated to cash before landing at Transamerica.

## Definition

Per `CSR%20Cash%20Report%20-%202025.md.txt`, the CSR Cash Report is the [[csr]] template used when the conversion strategy is cash-in. It summarizes the asset transfer, election handling, fund/source/suspense account activity, and any outstanding conversion items for the plan sponsor.

## Contents

- **Asset transfer overview** — dollar amounts, dates, refs
- **Investment and contribution election handling** — how participant elections were applied to the incoming cash
- **Fund, source, and suspense account summaries** — where money landed
- **Outstanding conversion items** — anything still unresolved at CSR issuance

## Why It Matters

The CSR Cash Report is the sponsor's official receipt that conversion completed. It documents the chain of custody for incoming assets, what elections were honored, and what (if anything) is still in flight. For cash conversions, the sponsor uses this report to reconcile against their prior vendor's final statement.

## Distinction from Mapping Variant

The [[csr-mapping-report]] is the parallel deliverable for mapping-based conversions (TIK or fund-level map). Choice of template is driven by the conversion type (see [[conversion-types]]).
