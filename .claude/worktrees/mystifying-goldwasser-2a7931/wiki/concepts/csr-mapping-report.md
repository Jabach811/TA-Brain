# CSR Mapping Report (2025)

The 2025 sponsor-facing Conversion Summary Report variant used for **mapping-based conversions** — where prior-vendor assets move in-kind or via fund-by-fund mapping rather than liquidation to cash.

## Definition

Per `CSR%20Mapping%20Report%20-%202025.md.txt`, the CSR Mapping Report is the [[csr]] template used when the conversion strategy includes fund-level mapping. It documents how each prior fund mapped to a Transamerica fund and summarizes the post-mapping transfers and reconciliations.

## Contents

- **Asset and election mapping strategy** — the overall approach
- **QDIA handling** — how participants without elections were defaulted (typically TDF; see [[target-date-chart]])
- **Fund and source mapping tables** — the prior-to-TA mapping matrix
- **Post-mapping transfers and reconciliations** — cleanup movement and final balances

## Why It Matters

Mapping conversions are more complex than cash because there is fund-by-fund correspondence to document and defend. The CSR Mapping Report is the sponsor's reference for every mapping decision. It backstops the [[audit-pack]] that the DC produces for internal audit.

## Distinction from Cash Variant

The [[csr-cash-report]] is the parallel deliverable for cash conversions. The two share structure but diverge in the middle — mapping includes the fund-by-fund matrix; cash does not.
