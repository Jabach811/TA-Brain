# Final Funds and Pricing Reminder

An email template used by [[com]] and Transition Managers to remind clients — or internal TMs — of upcoming or past-due final funds-and-pricing deadlines.

## Definition

Per `Final%20Funds%20and%20Pricing%20Reminder%20email%20template.md.txt` and `Email%20to%20TM's.md.txt`, this is a paired set of email templates: one for sending to the client, and one for the internal status check to the Transition Manager before escalation. Both exist to keep the final funds-and-pricing milestone visible and to document the path to escalation if the deadline slips.

## Key Messages

- Importance of final pricing to conversion success
- Risks of missing deadlines — go-live slippage, client-facing delays
- Guidance on escalation if deadlines cannot be met

## Internal TM Variant

The internal version asks TMs to:
- Review plan status
- Respond with updates prior to escalation to Sales and Pricing

This is the middle step before formal escalation — a paper trail the COM can point to when decisions are made.

## Why It Matters

Final funds-and-pricing is a hard gate for go-live: the blackout calendar, liquidation timing, and mapping audits all depend on it being locked. A missed deadline cascades through every downstream step. The reminder template standardizes the language and makes the consequences explicit so clients and TMs both understand the stakes.
