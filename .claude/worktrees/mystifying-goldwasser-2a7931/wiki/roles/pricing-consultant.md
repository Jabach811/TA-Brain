# Pricing Consultant

Reviews pricing during the Transfer of Assets Agreement (TOA) workflow.

## What This Role Does

The Pricing Consultant is one of the four key roles named in the [[toa]] procedures (per TOA_Procedures.md.txt):

1. [[sales-consultant]] — initiates the TOA
2. **Pricing Consultant** — reviews pricing
3. Transition Manager ([[com]])
4. Plan Service Consultant ([[psc]])

The Pricing Consultant's specific work is pricing review: confirming that the pricing structure agreed on during sales is accurate, feasible, and aligned with Transamerica's pricing standards before the TOA advances.

## Key Handoffs

**Comes in:** Draft TOA with pricing section from [[sales-consultant]]

**Goes out:** Pricing-reviewed TOA to [[com]] (Transition Manager) for continued workflow
