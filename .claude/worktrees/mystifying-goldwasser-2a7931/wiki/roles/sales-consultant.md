# Sales Consultant

Initiates the Transfer of Assets Agreement (TOA) — the starting role in the TOA lifecycle.

## What This Role Does

The Sales Consultant creates the initial TOA that begins the New Business onboarding workflow. Per TOA_Procedures.md.txt, the TOA moves through four roles in sequence: Sales Consultant → Pricing Consultant → Transition Manager ([[com]]) → Plan Service Consultant ([[psc]]).

The Sales Consultant is upstream of every other role documented in this wiki for a New Business plan — no DC, COM, PLC, or QC engagement begins until a TOA exists.
