§ 02   Process

How it moves.

A four-part process that loops. We rarely do it in order. We always do it all.

Phase 01 Immerse

Listen first.

Every engagement begins in the field. We shadow, interview, map the territory, and bring back raw material — not conclusions — to the studio.

Field research Interviews Ethnography Data synthesis
Phase 02 Frame

Define the question.

The hardest work is choosing what to solve. We write the brief we wish we'd been given — sharp, specific, and worth the team's attention.

Problem framing Narrative Scope Success metrics
Phase 03 Build

Make it real.

Sketch, prototype, test, rewrite. We ship early and often internally so that the thing that meets the world has already been shaped by contact with it.

Prototyping Critique Engineering Iteration
Phase 04 Ship

Release, measure, evolve.

Launch is the beginning, not the end. We measure what matters, keep the hotline open, and return to the work with what we learned.

Launch Monitoring Post-mortem Next loop
