## Description

When someone covers a wire, make clear whether the money needs to be placed into Advanced Employer and what reference/contact details they need.

## Current Updates

### Why It Matters

Pella needed Jessica to cover the wire while Joel was out. The key handoff was making sure she could put the wire into Advanced Employer and had the right contacts.

### Checklist

- [ ] Confirm covering person.
- [ ] Confirm wire date.
- [ ] Provide client contact.
- [ ] Provide vendor/prior RK contact.
- [ ] Provide estimated amount if available.
- [ ] Confirm Advanced Employer handling.
