# Reminder Rules

## Severity Levels

| Severity | Meaning | Reminder Timing |
|---|---|---|
| S1 Critical | Money movement, go-live, hard deadline, client-blocking issue | Same day until resolved |
| S2 High | Due soon, final files, deferral report, setup blocker | Next business day if quiet |
| S3 Medium | Follow-up this week, meeting prep, process risk | 2-3 business days if quiet |
| S4 Low | Cleanup, documentation, nice-to-have | Weekly review |

## Stale Item Rule

If an open item has not been mentioned within its reminder timing window and is not marked resolved, surface it in `open-reminders.md`.

## Resolution Rule

When Joel says an item is done, resolved, wrapped, sent, received, or no longer needed:

1. Move it from open items to resolved items on the related page.
2. Remove or mark it resolved in `open-reminders.md`.
3. Add the resolved date.

## Reminder Tone

Reminder text should be plain and useful:

> You have not mentioned Pella final files since 2026-05-05. It is still marked open. Worth a quick follow-up?
