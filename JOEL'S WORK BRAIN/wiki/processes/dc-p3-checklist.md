## Rule

When P3 is available for a plan, run a quick standardized DC check before the work drifts.

Joel identified that early P3 checks can catch missing setup details before they turn into downstream cleanup. Leiby is the current seed plan for this checklist.

- [ ] Confirm classes are present.

- [ ] Confirm escalation settings.
- [ ] Check any other plan-specific setup areas.
- [ ] Record gaps on the plan page.
