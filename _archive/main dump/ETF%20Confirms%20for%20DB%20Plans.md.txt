# EFT Confirms for DB Plans

## Purpose
- Enable generation and printing of EFT confirms for Defined Benefit (DB) plans when requested by the client.

## Key Notes
- Not mailed by default. Requires client request and manager approval.
- TC submits request via Serena ticket.

## Ticket Requirements
- Project: Database Change
- Application: Plus
- Enable EFT Confirms effective with first payment.
