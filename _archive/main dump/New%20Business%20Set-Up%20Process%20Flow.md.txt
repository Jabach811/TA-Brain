# New Business Set-Up Process Flow

## Purpose
- Define plan participant set-up responsibilities within the New Business process.

## Key Roles
- Plan Consultant (PLC)
- Transition Manager (TM)
- Data Consultant (DC)
- Communications Consultant (CC)

## Core Activities
- Prior Plan Review (PPR) preparation and review
- PARIS III system setup
- Plan Service Checklist completion and sign-off
- Funding document processing and authorization
