# Pre-Conversion Loan Analysis Procedures

## Purpose
- Perform loan due diligence prior to conversion.

## Data Options
### Option 1
- Outstanding principal
- Repayment amount and frequency
- Interest rate
- Actual maturity date
- Loan default date (if available)

### Option 2
- Outstanding principal
- Repayment amount and frequency
- Interest rate
- Issue date
- Loan term (months or years)
- Loan default date (if available)

## Process
- Enter data into Pre-Conversion Loan Analysis Worksheet.
- Populate formulas and review next payment due dates.
- Report loans past due by more than 30 days.
