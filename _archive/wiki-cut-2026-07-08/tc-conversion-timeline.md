# TC Conversion Timeline

Working timeline for TC-owned and TC-supported activities during a plan conversion.

## Overview

The TC conversion timeline is a personal operating checklist for keeping administrative setup, audits, participant access, notices, payroll support, and closeout from drifting during a conversion. It is not just a task list; it is the bridge between plan setup, participant-facing readiness, and handoff to the ongoing team.

> [!warning] Source status
> This page combines legacy TC material with Dan's TC role summary. Validate timing, owners, and required artifacts against current team procedure before using it as a live checklist.

## Trigger

The timeline starts when a new conversion is assigned and the TC is brought into internal/new-sale planning.

## Steps

1. **Join project initiation calls.** Owner: TC. Attend internal new sale, introduction, pricing, PRD, submission package, weekly internal, and weekly external calls where requested.
2. **Back up COM on administration questions.** Owner: TC with COM. Help COM respond to day-to-day administration questions and conversion-related inquiries from Processing or Customer Contact Center.
3. **Review and maintain the subpack.** Owner: TC. Take over the submission package after the preliminary draft, circulate revisions after the new business conference call, and keep updates current through project completion.
4. **Create outsourcing and special-service setup items.** Owner: TC. Create Outsourcing Addendum documents, verify review for accuracy, and submit setup requests for PX, CMP, MA, CPMA, statements, logos, non-dynamic forms, or other special administrative services.
5. **Support SDA/PCRA work if applicable.** Owner: TC with prior carrier/PCRA contacts. Track asset breakdowns, letters of authorization, trading menus, conference calls, and acceptable asset reviews.
6. **Complete first system audit and notices setup.** Owner: TC with QC. Create entity groups for PoD material generation, run/review notices, review the Fee Disclosure Notice with the proper checklist, confirm required P3 tabs such as Report Output, Outsourcing, and Remittance, and send completion email to the conversion team.
7. **Review initial notices and enrollment materials.** Owner: TC with CE/COM. Complete the full system audit, review the initial notice, and notify CE so enrollment materials can be reviewed with the client.
8. **Prepare participant and contact-center readiness.** Owner: TC. Complete transactions allowed spreadsheet, new business page, case notes, and internal materials needed by Contact Center, Remit Loaders, Processors, AM, or other downstream teams.
9. **Coordinate plan sponsor administration materials.** Owner: TC. Create remittance guides, administration manuals, call recaps, RDD forms, PSD training, WX access/user manuals, and administrative procedure documentation.
10. **Coordinate eligibility/enrollment readiness.** Owner: TC with QC and DC. Request setup or review of enrollment and eligibility tabs, collaborate on eligibility services and enrollment kit mailings, run/review enrollment kits, and confirm final material readiness.
11. **Activate and audit PSD/DDOL/VRU.** Owner: TC with QC. Activate plan sponsor access, add client contacts, audit test participants, audit live participants, audit the Participant Website with a test participant account, and verify DDOL/VRU/PSD setup, testing, and activation.
12. **Support mapping and data readiness.** Owner: TC with DC. Complete fund mapping and source mapping work where TC owns the administrative/provision attributes.
13. **Complete day-of-wire and live-participant audits.** Owner: TC with QC/DC. Confirm conversion records/assets and participant website readiness before access changes.
14. **Run go-live controls.** Owner: TC. Complete the [[go-live-checklist]], schedule go-live meeting, and notify required parties once the plan is live.
15. **Finish post-go-live cleanup.** Owner: TC. Term kit cleanup, statement audit, final system audit, conversion statement request, and final statement hold release where applicable.
16. **Close out conversion.** Owner: TC. Complete transition call checklist, schedule and facilitate conversion closure calls with internal parties, hold transition call with ongoing team, add final subpack to AWD item, and update [[nbi]] with transition date.

## Handoff Points

- TC receives plan setup and project context from COM/TM.
- TC receives data and mapping inputs from [[roles/lm-dc]].
- TC coordinates audit/control checkpoints with [[qa]].
- TC provides admin readiness details to Contact Center, Remit Loaders, Processors, AM, and ongoing account teams.
- TC hands the final administrative package to the ongoing team at transition closeout.

## Timing / Deadlines

- Special service tickets and notice setup should be started early because they can block participant communications and access.
- Outsourcing Addendum review, P3 tab setup, and Fee Disclosure Notice review need enough runway to be corrected before initial notice/enrollment material review.
- PSD/DDOL/VRU testing must happen before limited or full access activation.
- Closeout should not happen until final system/audit tasks, statement items, and NBI transition updates are complete.

## Failure Modes

- Subpack revisions are not maintained after the first draft.
- Special services are requested late and block notices, enrollment material, or go-live.
- Outsourcing addenda, P3 tabs, or Fee Disclosure Notice review are treated as paperwork instead of setup controls.
- CE/COM reviews enrollment materials before TC has finished the full system audit and initial notice review.
- PSD/DDOL/VRU is activated before test participant audit is complete.
- Case notes are incomplete, leaving the Contact Center or ongoing team without the plan-specific rules they need.
- Final subpack or transition date is not added to NBI/AWD, leaving the conversion administratively open.
