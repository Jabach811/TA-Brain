# Case Notes Template

Structured plan-level notes used to preserve conversion and administration rules for downstream teams.

## Definition

The case notes template is a standardized plan note structure. It tells Contact Center, processing, ongoing administration, and conversion teams what is unusual, what participants may do, what plan-specific rules apply, and where risk lives.

> [!warning] Template language
> The source contains draft/template language with placeholders and plan-specific choices. Do not paste it into live case notes without cleaning placeholders and validating the current plan strategy.

## Why It Matters

Case notes are the operating memory of the plan. They protect downstream teams from guessing about blackout dates, asset transfer restrictions, Financial Guide/CPMA availability, deferral handling, source groups, information sharing, asset gathering, web portal rules, withdrawal exceptions, and participant-facing instructions.

## High-Risk Note Areas

- **Special Alerts:** effective dates, blackout start/end, limited access, CPMA/Financial Guide warnings, asset acceptance limitations, and managed advice routing.
- **Allocations:** source groups, mapped allocations, service-only funds, and CPMA restrictions.
- **Deferrals:** mapped deferrals, Master Administrator/Common Remitter handling, and participant-facing election rules.
- **Information Sharing:** loan and hardship certification process.
- **Funds:** service-exclusive funds and service stoppage/replacement fund behavior.
- **Web Portal:** Financial Guide SSO, participant issue triage, and web access notes.
- **Withdrawals:** special approval boxes, QDRO approvers, guaranteed pooled fund back-end load notes, and plan-specific exceptions.
