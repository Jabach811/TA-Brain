# Team Charters

Legacy role-boundary documents describing who owns what across conversion project types.

> [!warning] Legacy source
> The source charter text says it is outdated and needs review. Use this page to understand historical role boundaries and handoffs, not as a current authority without QC.

## Definition

The team charters define responsibilities and expectations for functional teams involved in plan installation work. The source includes separate charters for DC plan conversions, start-up projects, external mergers, and internal mergers.

## Why It Matters

The charters are useful because they show how the conversion work is meant to be divided: sales/pricing feed the project, COM/TM coordinates the timeline and client communication, DC owns data movement and payroll files, TC owns administrative setup and controls, QC audits conversion data and system setup, and the plan sponsor/client provides information and approvals.

Even if the details are stale, the charters help explain why conversion work fails at handoff points rather than inside one isolated role.

## Evidence / Examples

| Project Type | Useful Signal |
|---|---|
| DC Plans | Full new-plan conversion with asset movement, data conversion, plan setup, TC audits, QC controls, and client-facing communication. |
| Start-up | Similar administrative setup without the same asset-conversion intensity; TC may be assigned only in some cases. |
| External Merger | More emphasis on mapping, participant transfer, communication, merger documents, and external prior-provider coordination. |
| Internal Merger | Adds internal data movement, outsourcing coordination, active population/new-hire handling, and internal data project management. |

## Role Boundary Pattern

- [[com]] / TM: project coordination, client communication, document routing, project plan, and escalation.
- [[lm-dc]]: data layouts, test/live files, payroll source coordination, conversion records, assets, wire summaries, source mapping collaboration, and data issue resolution.
- [[tc]]: administrative procedure setup, case notes, remittance/admin materials, PSD training, DDOL/VRU/PSD activation, audit controls, and conversion closeout.
- [[qa]] / QC: conversion audit, default allocation/deferral checks, census detail review, loan analysis, statement support, website test account support, and financial-risk checks.
- Plan Sponsor / Client: provides data, approvals, documents, meeting participation, payroll coordination, and implementation decisions.
