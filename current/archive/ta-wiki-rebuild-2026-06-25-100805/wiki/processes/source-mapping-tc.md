# Source Mapping TC Procedure

TC-supported procedure for turning prior-vendor source information into an administratively usable source mapping.

## Overview

This process shows the division of labor between DC and TC during source mapping. DC identifies source balances and gathers prior-vendor information; TC reviews the source attributes against the prior plan review and fills in administrative details.

> [!warning] Source status
> Built from legacy TC notes. Use alongside the current [[source-mapping]] page and validate against active workflow.

## Trigger

The process starts when DC has identified all sources with balances at the current vendor and saved the working spreadsheet in the account folder.

## Steps

1. **Identify sources with balances.** Owner: [[lm-dc]]. DC identifies all sources with balances at the current vendor and saves the information in a spreadsheet.
2. **Notify TC.** Owner: [[lm-dc]]. DC tells TC the spreadsheet is ready for review.
3. **Review prior plan source attributes.** Owner: [[tc]]. TC reviews sources against the prior plan review.
4. **Populate source attributes.** Owner: [[tc]]. TC notes source name to map to, contribution type code, contribution frequency, withdrawal provisions, and vesting.
5. **Resolve unidentified sources.** Owner: [[tc]] and [[lm-dc]]. If frozen or prior-plan sources are not identified in the prior plan review, TC asks DC to obtain attributes from the prior vendor.
6. **Complete the spreadsheet.** Owner: [[tc]]. TC updates the spreadsheet with findings.
7. **Share the completed source mapping.** Owner: [[tc]]. TC shares the completed information with TM/COM.

## Handoff Points

- DC hands source-balance identification to TC.
- TC hands administrative attributes and completed mapping back into the project channel.
- Prior vendor may be needed when prior-plan review materials do not identify frozen or legacy sources.

## Timing / Deadlines

Source mapping depends on test/prior-vendor information. Delays here can compress final data loading, participant setup, and audit time.

## Failure Modes

- Frozen sources are not identified and get mapped without verified attributes.
- Withdrawal provisions or vesting attributes are missing.
- DC and TC both assume the other owns prior-vendor follow-up.
- Spreadsheet is completed but not routed back through the project owner.
