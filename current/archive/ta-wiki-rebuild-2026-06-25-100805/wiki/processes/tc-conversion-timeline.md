# TC Conversion Timeline

Working timeline for TC-owned and TC-supported activities during a plan conversion.

## Overview

The TC conversion timeline is a personal operating checklist for keeping administrative setup, audits, participant access, notices, payroll support, and closeout from drifting during a conversion. It is not just a task list; it is the bridge between plan setup, participant-facing readiness, and handoff to the ongoing team.

> [!warning] Source status
> This page is built from legacy TC material. Validate timing, owners, and required artifacts against current team procedure before using it as a live checklist.

## Trigger

The timeline starts when a new conversion is assigned and the TC is brought into internal/new-sale planning.

## Steps

1. **Join project initiation calls.** Owner: TC. Attend internal new sale, introduction, pricing, PRD, and submission package calls where applicable.
2. **Review and maintain the subpack.** Owner: TC. Take over the submission package after the preliminary draft, circulate revisions, and keep it current through completion.
3. **Identify special services.** Owner: TC with QC/Prod Support. Request tickets for PX, CMP, MA, CPMA, statements, logos, non-dynamic forms, or other special administrative services.
4. **Support SDA/PCRA work if applicable.** Owner: TC with prior carrier/PCRA contacts. Track asset breakdowns, letters of authorization, trading menus, conference calls, and acceptable asset reviews.
5. **Complete first system audit and notices setup.** Owner: TC with QC. Create entity groups, run/review notices, check fee disclosures/QDIA notices, and send completion email to the conversion team.
6. **Prepare participant and contact-center readiness.** Owner: TC. Complete transactions allowed spreadsheet, new business page, case notes, and internal materials needed by Contact Center or processing teams.
7. **Coordinate plan sponsor administration materials.** Owner: TC. Create remittance guides, administration manuals, call recaps, RDD forms, PSD training, and administrative procedure documentation.
8. **Coordinate eligibility/enrollment readiness.** Owner: TC with QC and DC. Request setup or review of enrollment and eligibility tabs, run/review enrollment kits, and confirm final material readiness.
9. **Activate and audit PSD/DDOL/VRU.** Owner: TC with QC. Activate plan sponsor access, add client contacts, audit test participants, audit live participants, and verify DDOL/VRU settings.
10. **Support mapping and data readiness.** Owner: TC with DC. Complete fund mapping and source mapping work where TC owns the administrative/provision attributes.
11. **Complete day-of-wire and live-participant audits.** Owner: TC with QC/DC. Confirm conversion data and participant website readiness before access changes.
12. **Run go-live controls.** Owner: TC. Complete the [[go-live-checklist]], schedule go-live meeting, and notify required parties once the plan is live.
13. **Finish post-go-live cleanup.** Owner: TC. Term kit cleanup, statement audit, final system audit, conversion statement request, and final statement hold release where applicable.
14. **Close out conversion.** Owner: TC. Complete transition call checklist, hold transition call with ongoing team, add final subpack to AWD item, and update [[nbi]] with transition date.

## Handoff Points

- TC receives plan setup and project context from COM/TM.
- TC receives data and mapping inputs from [[lm-dc]].
- TC coordinates audit/control checkpoints with [[qa]].
- TC provides admin readiness details to Contact Center, Remit Loaders, Processors, and ongoing account teams.
- TC hands the final administrative package to the ongoing team at transition closeout.

## Timing / Deadlines

- Special service tickets and notice setup should be started early because they can block participant communications and access.
- PSD/DDOL/VRU testing must happen before limited or full access activation.
- Closeout should not happen until final system/audit tasks, statement items, and NBI transition updates are complete.

## Failure Modes

- Subpack revisions are not maintained after the first draft.
- Special services are requested late and block notices, enrollment material, or go-live.
- PSD/DDOL/VRU is activated before test participant audit is complete.
- Case notes are incomplete, leaving the Contact Center or ongoing team without the plan-specific rules they need.
- Final subpack or transition date is not added to NBI/AWD, leaving the conversion administratively open.
