Liquidation Day Featured article

From the TA Brain wiki  ·  Last updated 2026-04-15  ·  3 sources  ·  12 references

Liquidation Day is the day a plan's prior record keeper liquidates (or transfers in kind) plan assets and sends them to TransAmerica. It is the highest-stakes day in a plan conversion: every action is sequential, time-sensitive, and cannot be tested end-to-end. The Data Consultant orchestrates communications between the prior record keeper, Cashiering, and internal systems, and processing cannot begin until the wire is confirmed received.

!
Cannot be tested
The Day of Wire workflow in Informatica cannot be run end-to-end as a test. You can preview and debug, but the live execution is the first real run. Double-check every input before executing.

Day before

Email Cashiering:

"Hey, I have a wire coming in for [Plan Name]. Expecting approximately $[amount]. Just a heads up."

Send to: shtaopscashieringinc@transamerica.com

Cashiering may already have a daily wire list with DC assignments, but send the email anyway. Better redundant than missed.

Email the prior record keeper:

"Just confirming we're good for tomorrow. Let me know if anything has changed."

Morning of liquidation day

Email the prior record keeper:

"Good morning — today's the day. Please send the wire and provide a fund breakdown and total as soon as possible. I can't move forward until I have the breakdown."

Once the vendor confirms the wire is sent, notify Cashiering:

"Vendor says wire is sent. Expected total: $[X]. Let me know when you see it."

Wire confirmation

Cashiering's wire pull schedule:

PullTime (CST)
17:00 AM
29:00 AM
31:00 PM
43:00 PM

Cashiering notifies the DC by 3:45 PM CST if the wire was received that day. If there is a delay, they reply to the DC's wire-notification email acknowledging it and confirming when the daily notifications are complete.

!
Late wires roll over
Wires received after 3:00 PM CST are deposited the next business day.

When confirmed, Cashiering emails:

"Wire received for [Plan Name]. Amount: $X. Transfer Reference Number: [XXXX]."

The Transfer Reference Number (trans ref number) is the unique ID for this wire transaction in the system.

Processing by conversion type

Cash conversion

After the wire is confirmed and the total matches the breakdown:

  1. Book as 4 = Remit — wire hits State Street, book using the same process as a normal contribution.
  2. Email NY Recon (so they can identify variances and piece together components).
  3. Move the money to the Advanced Employer (AEDA) account in P3.
  4. Email all parties: "Wire received for [Plan Name], $[X]. Posted to Advanced Employer."
  5. Parties do their checks; they will confirm or flag issues.
!
Do not process participant balances today
For cash conversions, participant balances wait for final files. See booking assets for the distinction between booking wires and AEDA disbursements.

Mapping conversion

After the wire is confirmed and the total matches the breakdown:

  1. Create trans ref numbers in P2/P3 — one per fund in the CONV file.
  2. Fill totals into the pre-prepared CONV file (fund amounts come from the vendor breakdown).
  3. Verify the dummy participant (SSN 999-00-00) is in the census. Do this now if not already confirmed.
  4. Import the CONV file → run the Day of Wire workflow in Informatica.
  5. Check P3: dummy participant should have balances; trades should be out.
  6. Run verification queries.

Transfer in kind (TIK)

Same as mapping for CONV file setup, with these differences:

  • CONV file has re-reg = Y for TIK funds.
  • Day of Wire creates a placeholder (no details, no trades).
  • Notify Matt O'Connell's team that assets are moving.
  • Shares will arrive at Fidelity — Matt's team will identify and apply them.

Wire total mismatch

!
Hold everything
If the wire total doesn't match the vendor's fund breakdown total, do not process. Contact the vendor for an explanation. Do not proceed until totals are reconciled and everything is in good order.
"We don't process unless it's in good order."

Pre-checks before running Day of Wire

Before importing the CONV file to Informatica, verify:

  • Plan is fully set up in P3
  • Trans ref numbers created (one per fund)
  • CONV file totals match vendor breakdown
  • Dummy participant exists in census (mapping only)
  • Fund mapping is approved and loaded
  • Source mapping is approved and loaded

Trans ref number

The transfer reference number is the unique identifier for the wire transaction. It is:

  • Provided by Cashiering when they confirm the wire
  • Used to create corresponding ref numbers in P2 for each fund (reflected in P3)
  • Referenced in the CONV file for each fund
  • Used to track the transaction throughout processing

Format: Date in YYYYMMDD followed by ~7–8 random alphanumeric characters.

P2 navigation — creating trans ref numbers

  1. Login screen → enter CORP07 (CORP plans) or tdatest (TDA plans)
  2. Job # → 2001
  3. Enter contract number and affiliate number
  4. Select Option 4 to add a ref number
  5. Trans code: 1006
  6. Proc code: 3

One ref number per fund in the CONV file.

Cutoff time

!
SSBT cutoff: 4:00 PM EST
The Fund Management Calendar must be updated and the notification email sent to all four distribution addresses before 4:00 PM EST on any day trades go out.

Wire confirmation from Cashiering is expected by 4:45 PM EST. If the wire is not confirmed by 4:00 PM, update the FMC to the next business day and send a follow-up notification email.

See Fund Management Calendar for the full procedure, email distribution list, and per-conversion-type details.

"Entering estimates is fine. Not entering anything is a problem."

Common remitter plans

If the plan is coded as a "common remitter," the standard Pre-Process option in P3's Remit Action dropdown will be absent. Use "Verify Batch After Funding" instead. See common remitter plans for the full alternative flow.
