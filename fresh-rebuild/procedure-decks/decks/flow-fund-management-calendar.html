Fund Management Calendar

Notify and record large trades before cut-off risk hits.

1Determine whether the trade type requires calendar notification.
2Identify the conversion type and which trade event is occurring.
3Enter trade details before the cut-off window whenever the file is accessible.
4Send the required notification email to the distribution list.
5If the calendar file is locked, send the email anyway and keep trying to update the file.
6For cash conversions, track the wire, Advanced Employer, and participant balance events separately.
7Exclude small-money trailing dividends when the procedure says calendar entry is not needed.
8Confirm the intended trade is documented before downstream processing.
