Payroll Handoff at Go-Live

Move unfinished payroll testing to Fiduciary Services cleanly.

1Confirm payroll testing status as go-live approaches.
2If testing is incomplete, submit the FILESPECRQ AWD ticket.
3Include plan details, vendor details, current status, open issues, and files already tested.
4Notify any internal DC helpers that the project is moving to Fiduciary Services.
5Update NBI to show the payroll project handoff status.
6COM notifies the Account Manager that testing was not completed during transition.
7If testing is underway, coordinate an introduction to the AM; if not started, AM initiates contact.
8Fiduciary Services continues the payroll work after go-live.
