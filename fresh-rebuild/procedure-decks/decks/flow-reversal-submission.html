Reversal Submission

Use the new form-first reversal path before opening AWD.

1Complete the Reversal Form with plan, participant, transaction, amount, and reason details.
2Submit the form and wait for the automated email response.
3Copy the block labeled for the reversal team from the email.
4Open the appropriate reversal AWD ticket type.
5Paste the copied email block into AWD comments.
6Attach supporting documents and approval evidence.
7Submit the AWD ticket for the reversal team to work.
8Track the ticket through completion and confirm the correction landed.
