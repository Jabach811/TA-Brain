Reversal Submission

The reversal process now starts with the Reversal Form. The automated email from that form becomes the structured reversal-team block that gets pasted into the AWD ticket comments.

Run path

Procedure sequence

01

Complete the Reversal Form with plan, participant, transaction, amount, and reason details.

02

Submit the form and wait for the automated email response.

03

Copy the block labeled for the reversal team from the email.

04

Open the appropriate reversal AWD ticket type.

05

Paste the copied email block into AWD comments.

06

Attach supporting documents and approval evidence.

07

Submit the AWD ticket for the reversal team to work.

08

Track the ticket through completion and confirm the correction landed.

Decision points

What decides the path

Is this the right transaction type for reversal?
Does the amount require a higher approval level?
Were loan-specific fields completed when relevant?
Are funds already returned, stopped, or voided if needed?
Is the automated email block in AWD comments?
Watchouts

Where this can bite

!

The old Plan Correction Checklist is retired.

!

The form alone does not initiate the reversal; AWD is still required.

!

Skipping the email block slows the reversal team down.

!

Approvals depend on amount.

!

Funds that need stopping or returning must be handled before submission.
