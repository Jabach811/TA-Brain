The problem we all feel

Knowledge lives in heads.
Then those people leave.

Every team has someone who "just knows." Every department has handoffs that only work because of relationships you can't see. When that person is on PTO, or moves on, the knowledge walks out with them.

01 · Silos

Departments don't know each other.

What Conversions does is a mystery to Compliance. Audit needs answers from Operations and ends up scheduling four meetings to find them.

02 · Turnover

The hard-won context disappears.

Years of "the way things actually work" go with the person. New hires inherit a job description, not the wisdom that made it work.

03 · Reinvention

The same questions get asked forever.

Process steps, contact people, system quirks, who-owns-what — answered ad-hoc, in DMs, then forgotten. We solve the same problems twice a year.
