The idea

Compounds forever.

No long meetings. No documentation projects. Just one short brain dump from one person, every once in a while — and a system that quietly weaves it into a wiki the entire company can read.

01 · BRIDGE

Bridge the knowledge gaps.

The senior who knows everything? Their head is the single point of failure. Five minutes of voice into the wiki turns one person’s lived experience into a page anyone can find.

02 · CONNECT

Unite the departments.

Lateral — not hierarchical. The wiki maps how work actually flows between teams: who hands what to whom, and where the friction lives. Org charts can’t do this.

03 · SEE

Highlight the key relationships.

Every name, every system, every handoff is its own page. Click any link and you’re looking at the network of people and tools that make a process actually run.

04 · REMEMBER

Capture the history that always gets lost.

Why do we do it this way? Who changed the process in 2023? The wiki preserves every version, every contradiction, every open question — nothing gets quietly overwritten.

05 · PULL

Pull virtually any report.

Onboarding guides, team maps, handoff diagrams, glossary lookups, contradiction reports. Ask once — the wiki assembles it from everything we’ve already written.

It’s a win on every floor.

One contribution helps everyone — and so does the next, and the next. Each card is a different person, getting a different thing, from the same five minutes.
