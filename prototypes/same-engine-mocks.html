eyebrow · same engine · two scales

The brain is just more profiles.

Profile Builder isn't a tool that feeds the brain. It IS the brain — at the smallest unit. One conversation builds a profile. A whole company building, every week, builds TA Brain. Same gesture, same structure, same engine. The only thing that changes is how many voices.

How to read these

  • A · Equation — most direct visual rhetoric. The "=" sign carries a lot of the meaning.
  • B · Echo — most poetic, most "feel-good." Doesn't make the structural argument as cleanly but is the most beautiful.
  • C · Looms — most explicit about the "same engine" claim because the engine block is literally drawn once and shared. Best if you think readers won't otherwise connect the dots.
