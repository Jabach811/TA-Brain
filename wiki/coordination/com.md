# Communications Coordinator (COM)

The project manager for every plan conversion and the DC's route to the client — anything that needs to reach the client, or come from the client, goes through COM.

## What they own

- The conversion project plan, meeting cadence, and overall timeline (uploaded to [[nbi]])
- All client-facing communication: notices, letters, approvals, go-live email
- Routing approvals and documents between the client and internal teams
- The client review/approval loop for anything the DC needs signed off externally

## What you hand them

- **Fund mapping and source mapping documents** — COM routes them to [[qa]] and [[tc]] for approval and returns the approval to you
- **Early access file template** — when COM requests it; COM routes it to the client and returns the completed file for you to load. Confirm to COM once loaded (COM then tells TC to activate)
- **Asset receipt confirmation** — on liquidation day, confirm to COM that assets were received and deposited, with the amount. COM emails the client the same day, so don't sit on it
- **Payroll test status** — pass/fail on vendor test files as COM coordinates the payroll calls

## What they hand you

- **Project plan** — at kickoff, alongside your NBI assignment
- **PRD or onboarding package** — plan provisions (eligibility, vesting, sources, classes) you need before specs
- **Completed payroll questionnaire** — COM sends it to the client and forwards the completed copy to you; you can't start payroll setup without it
- **Mapping approvals** — returned from QA/TC via COM
- **Completed early access file** — from the client, for you to load

## When to contact them / escalation

- Anything that needs client action or a client answer — never go to the client directly
- Timeline slippage: COM owns the schedule and is the first escalation for a conversion running behind
- On liquidation day, stay in active contact so the asset-received email goes out promptly

## Common issues

- Client is slow returning the payroll questionnaire or early access file — flag to COM early; they chase the client
- Mapping approval stalls because COM hasn't routed it — follow up; a delay here compresses source mapping and CONV file prep
- COM has many parallel workstreams (notices, plan documents, vendor forms) that don't touch you — if a request isn't about data, mapping, payroll, early access, or assets, it's probably not yours
