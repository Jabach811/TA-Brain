# TC Details from Dan

Role summary for the Transition Consultant, focused on administrative support, operational continuity, recordkeeping setup audits, notice controls, participant website audit, and DDOL/VRU/PSD readiness.

## Summary

This source describes the TC as the role that establishes and communicates administrative processes during installation. The TC makes sure day-to-day administrative functions work properly and that the Plan Administrator understands the processes needed to operate the plan with Transamerica.

The source is especially useful because it gives a clean role-level description of TC ownership, rather than only a checklist view. It confirms that TC work is both administrative support and control work: supporting COM on admin questions, maintaining setup documentation, auditing P3 and participant website readiness, coordinating DDOL/VRU/PSD setup, and closing the project with internal parties.

## Key Claims

- TC acts as internal backup to COM on day-to-day administration issues and helps COM respond to administration questions.
- TC participates in weekly internal and external calls when requested.
- TC creates Outsourcing Addendum documents and makes sure they are reviewed for accuracy.
- TC maintains the Submission Package after the preliminary draft, circulates revisions after the new business conference call, and keeps updates moving through project completion.
- TC submits setup requests for special services such as PX, CMP, and MA.
- TC gives Remit Loaders, Processors, Contact Center, AM, and other internal parties the information needed to administer the plan correctly.
- TC provides plan sponsors with WX access and user manuals.
- TC establishes and documents any special administrative procedures required for the plan.
- TC works with COM/CE on special administrative forms when needed.
- TC supports conversion-related questions from Processing and Customer Contact Center.
- TC maintains documentation for client inquiries, administrative issues, special processes, and procedures.
- TC partners with ongoing support teams so they stay informed on relationship status and milestone action items.
- TC creates and maintains case notes during implementation.
- TC schedules and facilitates conversion closure calls with internal parties.
- TC creates and maintains the TC Conversion Action Plan.
- TC conducts and documents a thorough P3 recordkeeping system audit.
- TC creates the entity group needed to generate PoD materials after recordkeeping setup is confirmed.
- TC reviews the Fee Disclosure Notice using the appropriate checklist.
- TC makes sure required P3 tabs are set up, including Report Output, Outsourcing, and Remittance.
- TC reviews the initial notice and notifies CE after the full system audit is complete so CE can review enrollment materials with the client.
- TC audits conversion records/assets under the TC Action Plan.
- TC audits the Participant Website using a test participant account and checklist.
- TC collaborates with Quality Team and Data Consultant on eligibility services and enrollment kit mailings.
- TC coordinates DDOL/VRU/PSD setup, testing, and activation.
